Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how the "Byte Sized Facts Uploader" application ("the App") handles data. The App is a private tool used solely by the owner of the Byte Sized Facts channels to publish their own videos.

What the App accesses

  • YouTube (Google) data: with the account owner's consent, the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status, and to read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Instagram / Meta data: with the account owner's consent, the App uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels and read basic account information of the owner's own business account.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. The App has no server, does not collect data from viewers or other users, does not sell or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Use of Google user data

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in Instagram / Facebook settings under "Apps and websites". Revoking access stops the App from working; locally stored tokens can be deleted by the owner.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to the App, contact us using the address below. Since the App stores no data on servers, deletion means removing the locally stored tokens, which we do on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: via the contact options on our YouTube channel @ByteSizedFactsHQ.
